What leaves your device

  • Open-Meteo (weather/wind): the lake's coordinates. Today, Plan a trip and the Wind on my lakes map layer send each of your starred or saved waters' coordinates, one request per water.
  • USGS Water Services (flow, level, water temp): a box around the lake.
  • Map tiles (OpenStreetMap / Esri): the map area you view, or the lake's area when you save it for offline.
  • Windy / MyRadar: only when you open those panels or links.
  • National Weather Service (weather alerts): the lake's coordinates, and on Today each starred water's.
  • USGS statistics (flow vs normal): the gauge's id.
  • RainViewer (rain radar): the map area you view, only while the radar layer is on.
  • California stocking and lake levels come from this app's own site (refreshed nightly from CDFW and CDEC), so nothing extra is sent.
  • Nevada stocking, fishing reports and lake levels come from this app's own site too (refreshed nightly from NDOW, USGS and Reclamation).
  • Oregon stocking, Recreation Report, lake levels, algae advisories and regulations come from this app's own site too (refreshed from ODFW, OHA, USGS, the Army Corps and Reclamation).
  • Regulations, toxic algae and fish advisories, ramps and facilities also come from this app's own site (refreshed from Title 14 CCR, the Water Boards, OEHHA and OpenStreetMap).
  • Plant calendars: if you subscribe, your calendar app fetches that water's public plant feed from this app's site.
  • Directions links open Google Maps with that spot's location, only when you tap one.

Catches, notes, photos, spots and depth data never leave the device. Sharing with your crew sends only what you pick, directly as a file or inside a QR code's link (the part after #, which browsers never send to a website).
